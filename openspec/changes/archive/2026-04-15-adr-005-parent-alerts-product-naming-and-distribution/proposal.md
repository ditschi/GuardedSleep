# Proposal

## Why

Parents must notice noise events reliably, and naming and store listings must reflect the privacy positioning. Migrated verbatim from ADR-005 (DECIDED 2026-04-15; distribution line superseded by ADR-021 §3); `design.md` is the original ADR.

## What Changes

- The child's audio is the noise alert, with vibration, screen on and the meter; no separate ringtone.
- Product name "GuardedSleep – Privacy Baby Monitor" and the engineering names.
- Store wording with searchable terms; Play and direct APK (F-Droid main `DEFERRED`, IzzyOnDroid at beta).
